About MegaChat

We're on a mission to help Africans earn real money online — one conversation at a time.

Our Story

MegaChat was born from a simple observation: millions of Africans have smartphones, free time, and a strong desire to earn — but few opportunities that don't require experience, capital, or leaving home.

At the same time, millions of people in the US, UK, Europe, and Asia are looking for friendly, genuine conversations. They're lonely, curious, and willing to pay for real human connection.

We built MegaChat to connect these two groups. Since launching in 2023, MegaChat has grown into Africa's #1 platform for getting paid to chat online, with over 80,000 active users across Kenya, Nigeria, Ghana, Uganda, Tanzania, South Africa, and beyond.

Our Mission & Values

Our mission is simple: to create dignified, flexible income opportunities for every African with a phone. We believe that where you were born shouldn't determine how much you can earn.

Everything we build is guided by four core values that shape how we treat our users, our partners, and each other.

Trust & Transparency

No hidden fees. No fake promises. Every withdrawal is processed instantly and every payout is verifiable. We earn your trust by keeping our word.

Speed & Reliability

When you earn money, you should get it — fast. Our withdrawal system delivers to M-Pesa, MoMo, and bank accounts within seconds.

Respect for People

Our chatters are real people building real futures. We treat every user with dignity, offer fair rates, and provide round-the-clock support.

Continuous Improvement

We ship updates weekly based on user feedback. Your suggestions shape our roadmap — because MegaChat is built with you, not just for you.
