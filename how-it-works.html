Pro Tips to Earn More

The best earners on MegaChat aren't necessarily the ones who chat the most — they're the ones who chat the smartest. Here's what actually moves the needle:

Build a strong profile

A clear, friendly photo and warm bio attract 3–5x more chat requests than a blank profile.

Chat at peak hours

Evenings in Europe and the US (7 PM–1 AM EAT) see the highest chat volume and best tip rates.

Ask open questions

"What was the best part of your day?" works far better than "How are you?" — it opens real conversation.

Be authentic, not scripted

Foreigners pay for real connection. Scripted lines get ignored; genuine interest gets repeat chats.

Collect good ratings

Positive ratings from clients unlock higher per-minute rates and priority chat request matching.

Accept gift chats

Some foreigners send tips and gifts during chats. Being friendly in these conversations can 2–3x your income.
