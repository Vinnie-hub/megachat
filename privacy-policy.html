1 Introduction

Welcome to MegaChat ("we," "us," or "our"). We operate the website located at megachat.site and related services (collectively, the "Platform"). This Privacy Policy explains what personal information we collect, how we use it, and the rights you have over your data.

By creating an account or using our Platform, you consent to the practices described in this policy. If you do not agree with this policy, please do not use our services.

Our commitment: We will never sell your personal information to third parties. We collect only what's needed to run the Platform and pay you for your work.

2 Information We Collect

We collect information in three ways: information you give us, information we collect automatically, and information from third parties.

2.1 Information You Provide Directly

  • Account Information: Your phone number, email address, display name, password, and profile photo when you register.
  • Identity Verification: National ID or passport details for anti-fraud verification and payment compliance.
  • Payment Information: Your M-Pesa, MoMo, Airtel Money, bank account, PayPal, or crypto wallet details for withdrawals.
  • Profile Data: Your bio, preferences, chat history, and any messages you send through the Platform.
  • Communications: Messages you send to our support team via WhatsApp, email, or other channels.

2.2 Information We Collect Automatically

  • Device Data: Your device type, operating system, browser, and unique device identifiers.
  • Usage Data: Pages viewed, features used, time spent on the Platform, and interactions with other users.
  • Log Data: IP address, access times, and error logs for security and debugging.
  • Location Data: General geographic location (country level) for compliance and fraud prevention.

2.3 Information from Third Parties

  • Payment processors (e.g., M-Pesa, PayPal) confirming your transactions.
  • Analytics providers (e.g., Google Analytics) offering anonymized usage insights.
  • Verification services confirming your identity.

3 How We Use Your Information

We use your information only for legitimate business purposes directly related to running the Platform:

  • To provide our services: Create your account, match you with chat partners, and enable chat functionality.
  • To process payments: Send your earnings to your chosen payment method (M-Pesa, MoMo, bank, etc.).
  • To verify identity: Prevent fraud, money laundering, and abuse of the Platform.
  • To improve the Platform: Analyze usage patterns to make the Platform faster, safer, and more enjoyable.
  • To communicate with you: Send account notifications, updates, security alerts, and customer support responses.
  • To enforce our Terms: Investigate violations of our Terms of Service and community guidelines.
  • To comply with the law: Respond to legal requests, court orders, or regulatory obligations.

We do NOT use your information to send spam, sell to advertisers, or make automated decisions that significantly affect your rights.

4 Information Sharing

We share your information only in the limited circumstances described below:

4.1 With Service Providers

We work with trusted third parties who help us operate the Platform — including payment processors (M-Pesa, MTN, PayPal), cloud hosting providers, analytics tools, and customer support platforms. These providers access your information only as needed to perform their services and are contractually required to protect it.

4.2 With Chat Partners

When you chat with a foreigner, they see your display name, profile photo, and bio. They do not see your phone number, email, real name, or payment details.

4.3 For Legal Reasons

We may disclose your information if required by law, court order, or government request, or if we believe in good faith that disclosure is necessary to protect the rights, property, or safety of MegaChat, our users, or the public.

4.4 Business Transfers

If MegaChat is acquired, merged, or sells assets, your information may be transferred as part of that transaction. You will be notified via email or a prominent notice on the Platform.

What we will NEVER do: Sell your personal data to advertisers, data brokers, or any third party for marketing purposes.

5 Data Security

We take data security seriously. MegaChat implements industry-standard security measures to protect your personal information from unauthorized access, alteration, disclosure, or destruction.

  • Encryption in Transit: All data transmitted between your device and our servers is encrypted using HTTPS/TLS 1.3.
  • Encryption at Rest: Sensitive data (passwords, payment details) is encrypted using industry-standard hashing and encryption algorithms.
  • Access Controls: Only authorized MegaChat employees can access personal data, and only when necessary to perform their duties.
  • Regular Security Audits: We conduct periodic security reviews and penetration testing.
  • Fraud Detection: Automated systems monitor for suspicious activity 24/7.

However, no system is 100% secure. If you suspect your account has been compromised, contact us immediately via WhatsApp.

6 Your Privacy Rights

Depending on your location, you have specific rights over your personal information. We honor these rights for all users, regardless of where you live:

  • Right to Access: Request a copy of all personal information we hold about you.
  • Right to Correction: Update or correct inaccurate information through your dashboard or by contacting support.
  • Right to Deletion: Request deletion of your account and personal data (subject to legal and tax retention requirements).
  • Right to Restriction: Request that we stop processing certain types of data.
  • Right to Data Portability: Receive your data in a machine-readable format (JSON or CSV).
  • Right to Object: Object to processing for marketing purposes (we don't do this anyway).
  • Right to Withdraw Consent: Withdraw consent for optional data processing at any time.

To exercise any of these rights, contact us via WhatsApp or email. We respond to all requests within 30 days.

Kenya Data Protection Act (KDPA 2019): If you are a Kenyan resident, you also have the right to lodge a complaint with the Office of the Data Protection Commissioner (ODPC) if you believe your rights have been violated.

7 Cookies & Tracking

We use cookies and similar technologies to enhance your experience and understand how the Platform is used. Cookies are small text files stored on your device.

7.1 Types of Cookies We Use

  • Essential Cookies: Required for login, security, and core Platform functionality. These cannot be disabled.
  • Preference Cookies: Remember your settings (language, dark mode, etc.).
  • Analytics Cookies: Help us understand how users interact with the Platform (via Google Analytics). All data is anonymized.
  • Performance Cookies: Measure page load times and error rates.

7.2 Third-Party Cookies

We use limited third-party services (Google Analytics, Cloudflare) that may set their own cookies. You can opt out of Google Analytics by installing the Google Analytics Opt-Out Browser Add-on.

7.3 Managing Cookies

You can control cookies through your browser settings. Disabling essential cookies may prevent you from logging in or using certain features. Most browsers let you block third-party cookies entirely.

8 Third-Party Services

Our Platform integrates with several third-party services to deliver the best experience. Each has its own privacy policy that we encourage you to review:

  • Payment Processors: M-Pesa (Safaricom), MTN MoMo, Airtel Money, PayPal, and crypto payment providers.
  • Analytics: Google Analytics (anonymized usage data).
  • Hosting & CDN: Cloudflare, Cloudinary (image hosting).
  • Communication: WhatsApp Business (for support and notifications).
  • Social Media: Facebook, Instagram (only when you interact with our official pages).

We are not responsible for the privacy practices of these third parties. If you have concerns, please contact them directly.

9 Children's Privacy

MegaChat is not intended for anyone under 18 years of age. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a minor has created an account, please report it immediately via WhatsApp so we can delete the account.

We may verify your age during registration using your national ID. Providing false information may result in account termination without refund.

10 International Data Transfers

MegaChat serves users across Africa, and our servers are located in secure data centers that may be outside your home country. When we transfer your data internationally, we ensure appropriate safeguards are in place:

  • Contractual clauses requiring the same level of protection we offer in your home country.
  • Encryption of data in transit and at rest.
  • Regular security audits of all data processors.

By using MegaChat, you consent to the transfer of your information to these jurisdictions, which may have different data protection laws than your own.

11 Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time to reflect changes in our practices, technology, or legal requirements. When we make material changes:

  • We'll update the "Last updated" date at the top of this page.
  • We'll notify you via email or in-app notification.
  • For significant changes, we'll request your renewed consent where required by law.

Your continued use of the Platform after changes take effect means you accept the updated policy. If you don't agree, you should stop using the Platform and delete your account.
